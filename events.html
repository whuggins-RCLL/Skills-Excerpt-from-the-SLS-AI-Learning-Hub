SLS Tech Club

Welcome to the SLS Tech Club, where we ignite the tech spark in every member of the Stanford Law School community. You are already a member.

Club charter: our tech adventure map

Thank you for being part of the SLS Tech Club, where we hope to ignite the tech spark in every member of the Stanford Law School community. We are all about exploring the latest and greatest in technology, sharing insights, and building our tech skills together in a fun, collaborative environment.

Mission statement

Our mission is simple: to create a space for the entire Stanford Law School community to come together, dive into emerging technologies, and have a blast while learning and growing.

Focus

While our primary focus is often the rapidly evolving world of generative artificial intelligence and its impact on law and society, our curiosity does not stop there. The club is an open forum for all technology-related subjects — from low-tech productivity hacks to the mind-bending possibilities of quantum computing, any topic is open for consideration. If it sparks your curiosity, it belongs here. Have suggestions for future Tech Clubs? Let us know.

Membership

You are already a member. Everyone at Stanford Law School who loves tech — or is just curious — is welcome. The more, the merrier.

Objectives

  • Monthly meet-ups: we gather once a month on Zoom to catch up, learn, and share. You do not have to attend every gathering — come and go as time permits, and skip an event altogether if a topic does not interest you.
  • Reading and discussion: dive into books, articles, podcasts, and other resources that keep us ahead of the tech curve, then discuss what we learned and what we can implement.
  • Tech demos: get hands-on with new gadgets, software, and tools.
  • Guest speakers: occasionally hear from tech experts as passionate about technology as we are. Most often we watch videos of experts speaking, but we leave room for live guest speakers too.

Governance

Will Huggins and Taryn Marks co-facilitate. But this is your club too. Got ideas for activities or topics? Bring them. Use the suggestion form, pinned to the channel at any time, or post to the #techclub Slack channel.

Share your passion. If you have a topic you are excited about and would like to lead a discussion or demo, we strongly encourage you to be a guest facilitator. It is a great way to share your expertise with the community. Reach out to Will or Taryn to get on the schedule.

Meetings and resources

Meeting schedule

We mix it up. One month we dive into a great tech read, the next we have a guest speaker or a hands-on demo. Variety is the spice of our tech life. Have thoughts on what you would like to discuss? Let us know.

Between meetings

Join the #techclub Slack channel and pop in with ideas, reactions, and new resources throughout the month. Let us keep talking.

Resource materials

We pick books, podcasts, resources, and articles that are not only relevant but genuinely interesting. Reading lists go out in advance, so you have plenty of time to get cosy with the material.

Interactive sessions

Let us keep it lively — discussions, questions, and practical applications. We want everyone to share their thoughts and experiences, and we are respectful of all opinions at all times. This is a safe space to voice your thoughts.
