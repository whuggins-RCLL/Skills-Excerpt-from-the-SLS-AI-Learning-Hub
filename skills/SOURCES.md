# Authoritative Guidance Used in the Redesign

Checked August 27, 2026. These sources informed the decision rules in the skills; the set does not reproduce their manuals or claim endorsement.

## Research and source evaluation

- [ACRL Framework for Information Literacy for Higher Education](https://www.ala.org/acrl/standards/ilframework) — supports contextual evaluation of authority, research as inquiry, scholarship as conversation, and searching as strategic exploration.
- [Crossref metadata retrieval](https://www.crossref.org/documentation/retrieve-metadata/) — supports bibliographic identity and DOI metadata checks.
- [Crossmark](https://www.crossref.org/documentation/crossmark/) — supports checks for corrections, retractions, updates, and version information while recognizing that no single service proves completeness.

## Reviews and reporting

- [PRISMA 2020](https://www.prisma-statement.org/) — reporting guidance for applicable systematic reviews and extensions; not a universal template for narrative reviews.
- [EQUATOR Network reporting-guideline library](https://www.equator-network.org/reporting-guidelines/) — helps identify design-appropriate reporting guidance.
- [APA Journal Article Reporting Standards](https://apastyle.apa.org/jars) — reporting standards for relevant quantitative, qualitative, and mixed-method research.

## APA Style

- [APA Style and Grammar Guidelines](https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines)
- [APA reference-list guide](https://apastyle.apa.org/instructional-aids/creating-reference-list.pdf)
- [APA guidance for DOIs and URLs](https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines/references/dois-urls)
- [APA bias-free language guidance](https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines/bias-free-language)

APA 7 is a default for this set, not a claim that it governs every discipline or venue.

## Publication integrity and venue selection

- [Think. Check. Submit.](https://thinkchecksubmit.org/) — practical due diligence for journals and publishers.
- [Principles of Transparency and Best Practice in Scholarly Publishing](https://doaj.org/apply/transparency/) — transparency signals for journal identity, governance, peer review, access, ethics, archiving, and fees.
- [Committee on Publication Ethics](https://publicationethics.org/) — publication-ethics guidance, including authorship and AI-tool issues.
- [ANSI/NISO CRediT Contributor Role Taxonomy](https://credit.niso.org/) — transparent description of research contributions without treating the taxonomy as an authorship test.
- [Office of Research Integrity definition of research misconduct](https://ori.hhs.gov/definition-research-misconduct) — reinforces the prohibition on fabrication, falsification, and plagiarism.

Always check the current official source and the requirements of the user's institution, discipline, funder, ethics body, and target venue.
