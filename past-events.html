Monthly meetings

Tech Club meetings

The Tech Club is open to the entire SLS community. The club charter is on the Events page, and the next meeting is on the event calendar.

Tech Club #6: Smartsheet, Airtable, and Gemini AI in Sheets

Date 5 Nov 2025 Time 12:00pm PT Location Zoom

An interactive exploration of Smartsheet, Airtable, and Gemini AI in Google Sheets — project management, flexible databases, and enhancing spreadsheet workflows with AI.

Tech Club #5: Discussion — AI First, Human Always

Date 14 Aug 2024 Time 10:00am PT Location Zoom

A discussion centred on the book AI First, Human Always, exploring its principles, surprises, challenges, and applications in the law school and library context. Questions covered the meaning of the title, unexpected conclusions, institutional adaptation, source evaluation, the concept of the “experiential age,” future visions, and telling reality from science fiction in AI.

Tech Club #4: AI Design Week share-out

Date 11 Jun 2024 Time 10:00am PT Location Zoom

Members shared experiences and discoveries from the AI Design Week challenge: identifying repetitive tasks or workplace challenges and exploring AI tools that could offer solutions. The emphasis was on experimentation and collaborative brainstorming.

Tech Club #3: How to assess and find AI tools

Date 3 Dec 2024 Time 11:30am PT Location Virtual

Methods for assessing new and current AI tools, resources used for evaluation, establishing criteria and best practices for assessment, and gathering feedback for future Tech Club topics.

Tech Club #2: Building a custom ChatGPT

Date 22 Oct 2024 Time 12:00pm PT Location Zoom

An interactive session building a custom GPT live. Members submitted ideas for useful or interesting GPTs beforehand, and the session demonstrated the process of creating a tailored AI assistant.

Tech Club #1: General discussion about AI

Date 17 Sep 2024 Time 11:00am PT Location Virtual

The inaugural meeting: a general discussion about artificial intelligence, setting the stage for future topics and community exploration.
