Most library professionals have used AI to answer a question or summarise a document. This session is about what changes when AI can break a goal into steps, choose its tools, work across several sources, and iterate — and where a human still has to stay in the loop.

The ten skills in the demo

The agent in the recording is these ten skills plus one page of instructions. They are discipline-neutral — nothing in them is specific to law — and they are built so a researcher can see and check the work: sources are verified rather than asserted, AI output is never treated as evidence, and the human keeps authorship.

Download all ten skills The agent instructions

One ZIP, ten skills inside. Unzip the set once, then upload each of the ten skill ZIPs without unzipping it — the platform wants the packaged file. Install all ten for a general research agent, or only the ones the work needs. Then paste in the agent instructions.

01 Start here

Research Support Guide

Use when you are not sure where to begin, or have several different research needs at once. Not meant to be run mechanically when the right destination is already obvious.

Download
02 Scope

Research Project Planner

Turns a broad topic, an emerging idea, a dataset, a call for papers, or a stalled project into a bounded and feasible research plan.

Download
03 Review

Literature Review Helper

Plan, document, conduct, or synthesise a narrative, scoping, systematic, rapid, or other evidence review — with the design named rather than assumed.

Download
04 Sources

Academic Source Searcher

Identify source types, develop searches, locate and evaluate sources, chase citations forward and back, document what was searched, and produce a traceable source brief.

Download
05 Argument

Argument Builder

Develop or pressure-test claims, evidence, warrants, assumptions, alternative explanations, counterarguments, and implications.

Download
06 Structure

Draft Revision Coach

Diagnose organisation, section purpose, claim sequence, evidence placement, repetition, transitions, and which structural revisions matter most.

Download
07 Citations

APA 7 Citation Checker

Verify source identity, claim support, quotations, locators, correction or retraction status, and APA 7 citation form — a resolving DOI is not proof the source says it.

Download
08 Prose

Scholarly Writing Editor

Sentence- and paragraph-level editing that preserves substantive meaning, disciplinary conventions, and the author’s voice.

Download
09 Publish

Publication Planner

Compare credible journals, conferences, publishers, repositories, or other venues and prepare a submission plan against the requirements that apply today.

Download
10 Translate

Public Writing Adapter

Translate completed or well-supported research for public, policy, practitioner, student, media, presentation, or newsletter audiences without flattening the evidence.
