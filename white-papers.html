Featured paper

Transformer architecture

Attention Is All You Need (2017)

Ashish Vaswani, Noam Shazeer, Niki Parmar, Jakob Uszkoreit, Llion Jones, Aidan N. Gomez, Lukasz Kaiser, Illia Polosukhin

Introduced the Transformer, an architecture built around attention rather than recurrent or convolutional layers. It made the large-scale models behind GPT, BERT, and much of modern generative AI possible.

2 papers
Network

Generative Adversarial Networks (2014)

Ian Goodfellow, Jean Pouget-Abadie, Mehdi Mirza, Bing Xu, David Warde-Farley, Sherjil Ozair, Aaron Courville, Yoshua Bengio

This paper proposed two neural networks—a generator and discriminator—that compete against each other, unlocking remarkably realistic image generation and a new field of generative modeling.

Auto-Encoding Variational Bayes (2013)

Diederik P. Kingma, Max Welling

This paper introduced variational autoencoders, blending neural networks with probabilistic theory to learn a structured latent space from which coherent new data can be sampled.

Language model foundations

3 papers
Language

Improving Language Understanding by Generative Pre-Training (2018)

Alec Radford, Karthik Narasimhan, Tim Salimans, Ilya Sutskever

The paper that began the GPT lineage demonstrated the effectiveness of unsupervised pre-training followed by supervised fine-tuning.

Explore →↗ (opens in a new tab) Few-shot

Language Models are Few-Shot Learners (2020)

Tom B. Brown, Benjamin Mann, Nick Ryder, Melanie Subbiah, Jared Kaplan, et al.

GPT-3 showed that scaling to 175 billion parameters enabled a model to perform many tasks from only a few examples.

Explore →↗ (opens in a new tab) Context

BERT: Pre-training of Deep Bidirectional Transformers for Language Understanding (2018)

Jacob Devlin, Ming-Wei Chang, Kenton Lee, Kristina Toutanova

BERT learned from text in both directions at once, using full context to set new records across language-understanding tasks.

Image generation breakthroughs

3 papers
Diffusion

Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models (2020)

Jonathan Ho, Ajay Jain, Pieter Abbeel

Established diffusion models as a leading approach to high-quality image generation through a progressive denoising process.

Explore →↗ (opens in a new tab) Latent diffusion

High-Resolution Image Synthesis with Latent Diffusion Models (2022)

Robin Rombach, Andreas Blattmann, Dominik Lorenz, Patrick Esser, Björn Ommer

Made image generation practical by running diffusion in a smaller, compressed latent space.

Explore →↗ (opens in a new tab) StyleGAN

A Style-Based Generator Architecture for Generative Adversarial Networks (2018)

Tero Karras, Samuli Laine, Timo Aila

StyleGAN produced realistic faces through an architecture that controls image styles at different layers.

Multimodal and vision-language models

3 papers
CLIP

Learning Transferable Visual Models From Natural Language Supervision (2021)

Alec Radford, Jong Wook Kim, Chris Hallacy, Aditya Ramesh, Gabriel Goh, et al.

CLIP connected text and images with robust visual understanding and became a critical component of later image-generation systems.

Explore →↗ (opens in a new tab) DALL-E

Zero-Shot Text-to-Image Generation (2021)

Aditya Ramesh, Mikhail Pavlov, Gabriel Goh, Scott Gray, Chelsea Voss, et al.

DALL-E showed that models could generate compelling, whimsical images directly from natural-language descriptions.

Aditya Ramesh, Prafulla Dhariwal, Alex Nichol, Casey Chu, Mark Chen

DALL-E 2 used CLIP representations to generate coherent, detailed images through a two-stage process.

Training and alignment innovations

3 papers
RLHF

Training language models to follow instructions with human feedback (2022)

Long Ouyang, Jeff Wu, Xu Jiang, Diogo Almeida, Carroll L. Wainwright, et al.

Introduced an influential RLHF approach that aligns model behavior with preferences supplied by human labelers.

Explore →↗ (opens in a new tab) Preferences

Deep reinforcement learning from human preferences (2017)

Paul Christiano, Jan Leike, Tom B. Brown, Miljan Martic, Shane Legg, Dario Amodei

A foundational proposal to use human preferences as a reward signal for teaching AI systems complex goals.

Explore →↗ (opens in a new tab) Reasoning

Chain-of-Thought Prompting Elicits Reasoning in Large Language Models (2022)

Jason Wei, Xuezhi Wang, Dale Schuurmans, Maarten Bosma, Ed Chi, Quoc Le, Denny Zhou

Demonstrated that prompting models with intermediate reasoning steps can substantially improve logic and mathematics performance.

Foundational supporting technologies

3 papers
Embeddings

Efficient Estimation of Word Representations in Vector Space (2013)

Tomas Mikolov, Kai Chen, Greg Corrado, Jeffrey Dean

Introduced Word2vec, an efficient method for learning word embeddings that capture semantic relationships.

Explore →↗ (opens in a new tab) Optimization

Adam: A Method for Stochastic Optimization (2014)

Diederik P. Kingma, Jimmy Ba

Introduced the Adam optimizer, which adapts the learning rate for each model parameter and became a standard choice for training transformers.

Olaf Ronneberger, Philipp Fischer, Thomas Brox

The encoder-decoder U-Net architecture became essential to image-to-image tasks and later diffusion systems.

Recent advances

3 papers
Constitutional AI

Constitutional AI: Harmlessness from AI Feedback (2022)

Yuntao Bai, Andy Jones, Kamal Ndousse, Amanda Askell, et al.

Proposed a scalable alignment method in which an AI model critiques and revises outputs using a written constitution.

Explore →↗ (opens in a new tab) Scaling

PaLM: Scaling Language Modeling with Pathways (2022)

Aakanksha Chowdhery, Sharan Narang, Jacob Devlin, et al.

Demonstrated the capabilities of a 540-billion-parameter language model trained with the Pathways system.

Explore →↗ (opens in a new tab) Multimodal

Flamingo: a Visual Language Model for Few-Shot Learning (2022)

Jean-Baptiste Alayrac, Jeff Donahue, Pauline Luc, et al.

A multimodal breakthrough that connected pre-trained vision and language models to process interleaved sequences of text and images.
